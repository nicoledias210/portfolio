Current work · Sales training impact

Can better training change what happens on the sales floor?

I analyzed how learning participation and consistency relate to vehicle sales performance. Across tenure groups, salespeople who trained more, and trained more consistently, also sold more vehicles.

The signal
More consistent learning. Stronger sales performance.The pattern held across tenure groups, creating a meaningful question at the scale of Hyundai’s dealer sales network.

Impact analysis

The program has received Brandon Hall recognition. After establishing the sales-performance signal, I expanded the work into a yearly impact analysis that connects training behavior, business outcomes, and customer-experience verbatims.

Side quest 01 · Community

Creating spaces to belong

I tend to notice when someone is standing at the edge of a group, and my instinct is to bring them in. Throughout my life, that has meant volunteering to teach children experiencing homelessness in India, organizing outreach and mission trips, leading cultural programs, serving as Grad Core Chair at USC, and now helping young adults find community at my parish. The setting has changed, but the reason I show up has not: everyone deserves to feel welcomed, valued, and at home.
